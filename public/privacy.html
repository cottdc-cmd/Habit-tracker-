🌱 Privacy Policy

Little Wins is a personal habit tracker used by one person, its owner. It is not offered to the public.

  • What it reads: daily activity, sleep, readiness and workout summaries from Oura; activity, workout and cycle data the owner sends from Apple Health; and the owner's own Google Calendar events.
  • Where it goes: into the owner's private Notion workspace, to show progress on the owner's dashboard.
  • Sharing: data is never sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Removal: the owner can disconnect Oura at any time from their Oura account, and delete stored data in Notion.
